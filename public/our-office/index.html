Our Office

At Winnetka Dental Arts, we have been creating naturally beautiful smiles since 1992.

We focus on creating beautiful smiles by unraveling the root cause of dental instability resulting from development issues, from previous dental or orthodontic procedures, or from a traumatic event. We take a holistic approach by evaluating the oral development, airway competency, and nutrition of patients from childhood through adult life. By coordinating the integration of the various dental specialties, we develop a cost-effective treatment plan uniquely designed to provide the health and naturally beautiful smile you desire.

We work on routine dental care as well as diagnose and treat breathing disorders such as sleep apnea, which can be responsible for ADHD, depression, gastric reflux, diabetes, obesity, heart attacks, strokes, TMJ, insomnia, and other health issues.
