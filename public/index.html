Our Dentist

George F. Warga, DDS

General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Warga has been in private practice for over 30 years in Chicago’s North Shore suburb of Winnetka, IL with an emphasis on prosthetics, implants, cosmetics, TMJ, restorative dentistry and occlusal (bite) function. In addition to restorative dentistry, Dr. Warga offers sleep apnea appliances, Invisalign®, Botox® for therapeutic and cosmetic facial treatments.
